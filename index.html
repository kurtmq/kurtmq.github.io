What drives me forward

Three core values that shape how I approach every project, personal or professional.

Curiosity

I’ve always been the person who can’t leave a “why” unanswered. When something sparks my interest, I dive in, researching, experimenting, and figuring it out piece by piece. Every new thing I learn gives me more ways to help, create, and solve problems.

Ingenuity

I enjoy solving problems in ways people don't expect, not just patching what's broken but finding a better route to the outcome. It's the thinking that's earned recognition for my work, and it's what I bring to every project.

Humanity

Technology is only as useful as the people it serves. The way I work is shaped by paying attention to whoever's actually using what I build, and by years of leading community projects where the human side mattered more than the tech. Good technology should make someone's day easier, not harder.
